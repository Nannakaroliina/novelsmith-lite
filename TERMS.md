## What Novelsmith Lite is, and what it isn't

Novelsmith Lite is an aid to planning your own novel. It is not a guarantee:

- **Its output comes from AI agents.** Premises, characters, outlines and critiques can be wrong, derivative or unsuitable. You decide what goes into your book and remain responsible for it.
- **It does not enforce anything.** Agents are instructed to leave your anchored decisions alone and to write only their own files, and the skills check anchored text after each agent, but nothing prevents a write. Keep backups or version control for your book.
- **The outline check is a careful reading, not a program.** It can miss problems.
