## Dialogue rules
- Tag with "said" / "asked"; action beats instead of adverbs.
- Characters interrupt, deflect, lie, and leave things unsaid.
- Per-character NEVER-SAYS lists live in book/bible/characters/*.md.

## Rate limits (per 1,000 words of narration)
<!-- Starting values; replace with the author's own baseline once it has been measured. -->
- Em dashes: ≤ 3
- "not X, but Y" constructions: ≤ 1
- Tricolons / lists of three: ≤ 1
- Sentence fragments for drama: ≤ 2
- One-line paragraphs: ≤ 2
- Similes: ≤ 1 per page (~300 words)
- Body-cue emotion clichés (jaw tightened, breath hitched, chest tightened, stomach dropped): ≤ 1
- Scene-closing "wisdom" sentences: 0

## Always
- Name emotions plainly when a character would know what they feel.
- Specific nouns over categories ("the Volvo" not "the car"; "pike" not "fish").
- Every scene ends on an action, a line of dialogue, or an image, never on a moral.

## Never
- Explain subtext the reader already got.
- State the theme in narration.
- Use default-AI names (see name list).
